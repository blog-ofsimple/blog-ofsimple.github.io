Routy Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-04-09

Contact: support@example.com

1. Overview

Routy is an on-device app for managing goals, routines, and todos.

Core app data is stored locally on your device.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications: used for routine and todo reminders.
  • Calendar: used to import calendar events into todos.
  • Reminders: used to import reminders into todos on supported iOS devices.
  • Files / Photo Library: used to import, export, or share .routy backup files.

3. Advertising

Routy uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may process certain data for advertising and related purposes.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@example.com
